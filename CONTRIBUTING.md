# Contributing

This repository (https://github.com/dabsfv/x402-seller-self-check) holds one plain-English post about x402 payments ([README.md](README.md)) and a self-check for your own test copy ([SELF-CHECK.md](SELF-CHECK.md)). Issues are open for two things only.

## 1. Reporting a problem with the post or the self-check

Open an issue if:

- a fact is wrong or out of date (please link the source);
- a self-check instruction did not work as written on your own test copy (say which step, which client and version, and what happened);
- something is unclear.

## 2. Answering the question at the end of the post

"What's been the hardest part of getting x402 payments working for you?" If you sell through x402, or are building a seller, an issue is the place to answer it. There is an issue template for it; choose it when you open a new issue. Say if you would rather not be quoted, and you will not be.

## Please do not

- **Do not post secrets.** No keys, no seed phrases, and no signed payment headers, even from a test network. A signed payment is a bearer credential.
- **Do not name a seller or a service as having a problem,** and do not post results of testing anyone's endpoint but your own. If you think you have found a real weakness in someone else's service, tell them privately; this is not the place. For the x402 packages themselves, follow the security policy in their own repository.
- **Do not post results from mainnet or real money.** The self-check is for a test copy on Base Sepolia only.
- No advertising and no pitches.

Issues outside these two purposes may be closed. Pull requests that fix a typo or a broken instruction are welcome; larger changes, please open an issue first.
